Policy summary

  • Recordings, real-time transcription, and meeting-content analysis are completed by on-device AI models.
  • Audio, transcripts, and AI processing data are not sent to the developer or any third-party AI service.
  • Your content is not used for model training, advertising, or cross-app tracking.
  • Related data remains on your device and can be viewed or deleted at any time.
  • AI-generated transcripts and summaries may contain omissions or errors and should be checked against the actual meeting.

03 / DATA & PERMISSIONS

Information the App processes

  • Audio recordings: captured only after you start recording.
  • Transcripts and voice notes: text created from your audio, titles, edits, bookmarks, and notes.
  • Microphone permission: required for recording.
  • Speech Recognition permission: used to convert speech into text on this device.
  • Files access: used only when you import or export audio, transcripts, or notes.

You may change permissions in iOS Settings. Denying a permission affects only the related feature.

04 / ON-DEVICE AI

AI and data processing

Record & Transcribe Offline uses on-device AI models to process meeting recordings, real-time transcription, and meeting-content analysis. Recordings, transcripts, and all AI processing remain on this device. They are not sent to the developer or any third-party AI service and are not used for model training. Related data is stored on your device, where you can view or delete it at any time.

AI-generated transcripts and summaries may contain omissions or errors. Please refer to the actual meeting content. Before recording, make sure you have obtained the consent of meeting participants and comply with applicable local laws.

AI 与数据处理说明

Record & Transcribe Offline使用设备端 AI 模型处理会议录音、实时转写及会议内容分析。录音、转写文本和 AI 处理过程均在本设备完成，不会发送至开发者或第三方 AI 服务，也不会用于模型训练。相关数据保存在本设备中，您可以随时查看或删除。

AI 生成的转写及摘要可能存在遗漏或错误，请以实际会议内容为准。录音前请确认已获得与会人员同意，并遵守所在地法律法规。

06 / YOUR CONTROL

View and delete your data

You can view and delete recordings, transcripts, summaries, voice notes, bookmarks, and other related data inside the App at any time. You can also remove local App data through iOS Settings or by deleting the App.

To access or delete support correspondence that you sent to us, email contact@zhilianxingtu.com with the subject “Record & Transcribe Privacy Request.” We will complete a verified request within 30 days, unless applicable law requires a different period.

07 / SECURITY

On-device security

Keeping meeting audio, transcripts, and AI analysis on your device reduces unnecessary data transmission. The security of this data also depends on your device passcode, operating-system security, backup settings, and the storage locations you choose. No device or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.
