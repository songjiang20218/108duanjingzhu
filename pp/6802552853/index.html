Policy summary

  • Recordings, ordinary transcripts, and voice notes are designed to remain on your device.
  • AI features are optional. Transcript content is sent only when you start an AI feature and give permission.
  • Selecting “Not Now” prevents AI transmission; local recording and transcription remain available.
  • You can withdraw AI permission in App Settings > AI Data & Privacy.
  • We do not use recordings or transcripts for advertising or cross-app tracking.

03 / DATA & PERMISSIONS

Information the App processes

  • Audio recordings: captured only after you start recording.
  • Transcripts and voice notes: text created from your audio, titles, edits, bookmarks, and notes.
  • Microphone permission: required for recording.
  • Speech Recognition permission: used to convert speech into text. Recognition may occur on-device or through Apple's Speech service, depending on language, device capability, and iOS settings.
  • Files access: used only when you import or export audio, transcripts, or notes.
  • Technical data: an App-specific device identifier, IP address, request time, and security-log information may be processed when an online feature is used.

You may change permissions in iOS Settings. Apple handles any online Speech processing under the Apple Privacy Policy.

04 / AI PROCESSING

AI features and third-party processing

When you request an AI summary, meeting minutes, title, key points, action items, bookmarks, rewrite, or similar result, the App may send the following data:

  • meeting or voice-note title;
  • transcript text and timestamped transcript segments;
  • language, length, style, and formatting choices;
  • your prompt, question, edits, action items, or bookmarks; and
  • limited device, IP, request-time, and security information.

This data is sent to our AI processing service and third-party AI service providers, including OpenAI, L.L.C. and its infrastructure processors, solely to generate the result, deliver the service, maintain security, and prevent abuse. The current AI meeting-minutes request sends transcript text, not the original audio file.

OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models unless the customer opts in, and default abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days. See OpenAI API Data Controls.

06 / CONSENT & CONTROL

Your choices

Before AI data is sent

The App displays a separate AI Data Sharing authorization. Data is sent only after you actively select “Agree and Continue.”

Refusal and withdrawal

Selecting “Not Now” prevents the AI request. You can later withdraw permission in App Settings > AI Data & Privacy; withdrawal blocks new AI transmissions.

Withdrawal does not delete historical data. To request deletion of information already processed, email us with the subject “Record & Transcribe Privacy Request.”

We will complete a verified access or deletion request within 30 days, unless applicable law requires a different period.

07 / SECURITY & TRANSFERS

Security and cross-border processing

We use HTTPS, access restrictions, service isolation, and log rotation intended to protect information. No system is completely secure.

AI and online speech processing may occur outside your country, including where OpenAI, Apple, or their infrastructure operates. We limit transfers to what is needed for the requested feature, service security, or legal compliance.

08 / OTHER TERMS

Recording responsibility, children, and legal disclosure

You are responsible for obtaining any consent required before recording or transcribing other people. Recording laws vary by location.

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect a child's personal information without authorization required by law.
